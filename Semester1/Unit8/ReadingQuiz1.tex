\section{Memory Bank}

\begin{itemize}
\item $P = W / \Delta t$ ... Definition of power, ratio of work done to time duration
\item $\vec{p} = m \vec{v}$ ... Definition of momentum
\item $\vec{p}_{f,\rm tot} = \vec{p}_{i,\rm tot}$ ... Momentum conservation
\end{itemize}

\section{Power}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Suppose you are on a camping trip.  (a) If your body burns 120 W as you sit comfortably and enjoy nature, how many kcal do you need to replace the energy spent after 2 hours? (b) Now suppose you were \textit{shivering} the whole time, because you need warmer clothing.  Shivering requires 425 W.  How many kcal do you need to replace the energy spent? \\ \vspace{2cm}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Momentum and Impulse}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A gas molecule has a mass of $20 \times 10^{-25}$ kg and an average speed of 350 m/s (Fig. \ref{fig:gas}).  What is the magnitude of the momentum in kg m/s? \\ \vspace{1cm}
\item Suppose this molecule collides with the side of the glass beaker, turns around, and flies off in exactly the opposite direction at the same speed.  What is the \textit{change} in momentum, $\Delta \vec{p} = \vec{p}_f - \vec{p}_i$?  (This is how we build up the \textbf{kinetic theory of gases}). \\ \vspace{1cm}
\item A common injury in car accidents is a neck inury, because the head is snapped back and forward during the impact.  (a) Show algebraically that $\vec{F}_{\rm net} = \Delta \vec{p}/\Delta t$.  (b) Given that $\vec{F}_{\rm net} = \Delta \vec{p}/\Delta t$, we see that $\vec{F}_{\rm net}\Delta t = m\Delta \vec{v}$.  The quantity$\vec{F}_{\rm net}\Delta t = \Delta \vec{p}$ is called \textit{impulse}. (c) Suppose the head weighs 4 kg, and the initial velocity of the head is 12.5 m s$^{-1}$, but the car crash brings it to a stop in 50 ms. What is the net force and the acceleration of the head? \\ \vspace{2cm}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Momentum Conservation}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Two molecules collide and stick together, forming one larger molecule.  Each molecule weighs $20 \times 10^{-25}$ kg.  One has a velocity of 350 m/s, and the other has a velocity of -350 m/s.  (a) What is the total initial momentum (adding the two momenta)? (b) What is the final speed of the big new molecule?
\end{enumerate}
