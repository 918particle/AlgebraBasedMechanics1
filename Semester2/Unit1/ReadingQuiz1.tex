\section{Memory Bank}

\begin{itemize}
\item $\Delta U = q\Delta V$ ... Relationship between change in potential energy, charge, and voltage.
\item $V(r) = k q / r$ ... Potential (voltage) of a point charge.
\item $V(x) = E x + V_0$ ... Linear relationship between voltage, electric field, and distance between two charge plates to form a capacitor.
\item $Q = C\Delta V$ ... Relationship between stored charge, capacitance, and voltage.  Capacitance has the unit of 1 Farad, or 1 C V$^{-1}$.
\item $I = \Delta Q / \Delta t$ ... The relationship between current, $I$, and charge.  Current has a unit of 1 Amp (A), or 1 C s$^{-1}$.  
\item $q_e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}$ C ... The charge of 1 electron.
\end{itemize}
